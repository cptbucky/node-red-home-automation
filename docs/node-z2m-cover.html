Neutral commands in, Zigbee2MQTT out

The zigbee2mqtt cover node (type home-z2m-cover; canvas label: its name, its friendly name, or “zigbee2mqtt cover”) translates a cover controller’s neutral commands into Zigbee2MQTT’s set and get messages, and the device’s state reports into neutral cover readings. It has no broker connection of its own: device messages arrive from MQTT in nodes, and its publications leave through an MQTT out node. Never Home Assistant.

Looking for wiring?

Follow Wire a zigbee2mqtt cover, then Wire a cover for the controller side.

Inputs

Input messages
FromMessageEffect
Cover controller, output 1msg.cover_command = {operation, position, …}set_position, open, close, stop and observe are translated. Other operations are ignored.
MQTT in on zigbee2mqtt/<device>The device’s state JSONA reading when it carries a position, a motion or an end state; otherwise ignored.
MQTT in on zigbee2mqtt/<device>/availability{"state": "online"} / "offline", or plain online / offlineAvailability.

Topics are shown with the default base topic; <device> is the friendly name. Messages on other topics are ignored.

Outputs

Two output ports
PortMessageConnect to
1 · To the devicemsg.topic and JSON text msg.payloadAn MQTT out node on the same broker, with no topic set.
2 · To the cover controllermsg.cover = {kind: "observed", position, state, motion, observedAt, battery, charging} or {kind: "availability", online}The cover controller’s input.

Fields other than kind and observedAt appear only when reported. observedAt is when the report arrived, never a time the device gives. battery and charging (from charging_status, as text) are passed on.

Settings

Editor settings and exact defaults
Setting / propertyDefaultMeaning
Namename · stringEmptyDisplay label.
Brokerbroker · mqtt-brokerNoneNode-RED’s MQTT broker node. With one, the node subscribes to its device’s topics (<base>/<friendly name> and its /availability (exactly these: a friendly name can contain /)) and publishes on them itself: output 1 sends nothing, and the MQTT in and MQTT out nodes around it are no longer needed (still wired, a device message on its input is ignored, with a trace, so nothing is doubled). Its own commands come back through its subscription, as through an MQTT in node before, so it still tells them from someone else’s. It reads its cover on each connect, as a Status node’s startup did through the cover controller. Needed for a new node; opening and saving the node offers the first broker in your flows. A node saved before 6.2 keeps using its MQTT in and out nodes, and needs no broker until you choose one (publishesThroughOutput, with no form row, true for it and cleared when a broker is chosen).
Base topicbaseTopic · stringzigbee2mqttZigbee2MQTT’s base topic, unless changed in its settings. It cannot contain + or #.
Friendly namefriendlyName · stringEmptyRequired. The device’s friendly name in Zigbee2MQTT. It cannot contain + or #.

Device topics and payloads

Messages sent
Controller commandTopicPayload
set_positionzigbee2mqtt/<device>/set{"position": <0-100>}, rounded and clamped
openzigbee2mqtt/<device>/set{"state": "OPEN"}
closezigbee2mqtt/<device>/set{"state": "CLOSE"}
stopzigbee2mqtt/<device>/set{"state": "STOP"}
observezigbee2mqtt/<device>/get{"position": ""}, or {"state": ""} once the device has reported a state without a position

Some devices can’t be read through …/get; they still report their position whenever it changes.

How a report is read

  • Position is position from 0 (closed) to 100 (open), as Zigbee2MQTT reports by default. Set invert on the controller, or the device’s invert_cover option, if yours runs the other way.
  • Motion comes from motor_state (opening, closing, stopped; pause reads as stopped), else moving (UP, DOWN, STOP), else running: false as stopped. A cover that reports none is confirmed by the controller’s settle time.
  • End state: with a position, open only at 100 and closed only at 0. Without a position, the device’s OPEN or CLOSE, but only once its motion says it has stopped. Set the controller’s Cover to Open, close and stop only for such a cover; one that reports no motion either cannot be confirmed, and the controller says so.

Availability

zigbee2mqtt/<device>/availability becomes msg.cover = {kind: "availability", online} on output 2. It needs Zigbee2MQTT’s availability setting on; without it the cover is taken as available.

Status

  • waiting for the device (grey ring) until the first reading.
  • online (green) or offline (grey ring) after an availability message.
  • After a report: the position, such as 60%, or the end state, or position unknown; while moving it is prefixed with the motion, such as closing · 60%.
