Take the room for a while, then hand it back

The scene node (type home-scene; canvas label: its name, or “scene”) is one scene, such as Bath or Exercise mode. While it is on, it holds some of a room’s lights on or off with the values it gives them, and pauses motion lighting. When it ends, by request, by its safety timer or by a message your flow sends when its own condition is met, it lets them go, and each room gives its lights back what they were. It reaches lights only through room lighting controllers, so it works with any light adapter and never commands a device through Home Assistant.

What it holds, and what it hands back

  • Which rooms: each light names its room by the room controller’s Area, and its intents and releases carry it; a room acts only on those for its own Area, so two rooms may each have a main. Set the Area on each room a scene uses.
  • Through the room: each light is held under the scene’s owner, so motion, wall switches without a person’s mark, and the circadian style leave it alone while the scene is on, on or off. See owners stack.
  • Handed back: when it ends, each room gives a light back what it was before the scene: on or off as it was, in the room’s style as it is now, or a person’s own values if they had set it.
  • Scenes stack: a newer scene holding a light another holds takes it, and hands it back to the older one when it ends. Ending the older one first leaves the light with the newer.
  • A person’s change stands: a person changing a light the scene holds makes it theirs; the scene’s end leaves it alone.
  • Motion: paused under the scene’s owner, renewed every minute, so it lapses within the motion policy’s maximum pause (10 minutes by default) if the scene node goes away. Keep that Maximum pause above a minute, or motion runs between renewals. Ending the scene lifts its pause; another owner’s pause stands. If the room went vacant during the scene, motion’s vacancy delay starts when the pause lifts.
  • Restarts: its state is kept in its node context. With a context store, a scene on when the flows stopped is still on when they start, with its timer; if its time ran out meanwhile, it ends as they start. Its lights aren’t asked again: the rooms still hold them.
  • Deleted while on: it lets its lights and motion go, when you deploy with “Modified nodes” or “Modified flows”, while the rooms and motion policies are still running. On a full deploy the rooms are stopping too, so its release is lost and its lights stay held: end the scene before you delete it, or change those lights by hand, which makes them a person’s. Its motion pause runs out by itself.

Home Assistant

With an area and Publish set to MQTT state and Home Assistant discovery, Home Assistant gets a switch named after the scene. It shows whether the scene is on, and switching it sends ON or OFF to home/<area>/scene/<name>/set: with a Broker, the scene reads it itself; without one, wire an MQTT in node on that topic into the scene. Its availability is home/<area>/scene/<name>/availability; set the MQTT broker node’s Birth, Close and Will messages as for the other publishing nodes (see Home Assistant). <name> in topics is the scene’s name in lower case with other characters as _: exercise_mode.

Status

  • on · 3h 59m left (green dot) while on, or on with the timer off.
  • off · ended by timer (grey dot), or by request, home-assistant, deleted or what the end gave.
  • off (grey ring) before it has run.
