Circadian lighting policy

Message shapes, outputs and settings for the node that works out how lights should look over the day and sends it to room lighting controllers as a style.

A style for the day, not a light command.

The circadian lighting policy works out a brightness and colour temperature for lights that nobody has set otherwise, and sends it to room lighting controllers as a style. In the palette it is circadian lighting policy (home-circadian-lighting-policy); on the canvas it shows its name, or “<area> circadian lighting”.

The lights rise to the daytime level over the hour after waking, stay there through the day, and wind down to the sleep levels over the three hours before bed. Sleep mode holds the sleep levels whatever the time. Times are the Node-RED host’s local time. One policy can style several rooms, or each area can have its own.

Looking for wiring?

Follow Room lighting to wire it into a room, and House schedule to share wake and bed times between rooms.

Inputs

Changes arrive as msg.lighting, or as MQTT set topics while it publishes (with a Broker, read from the broker itself).

Input messages
MessageFieldsMeaning
lighting.kind = "circadian" or "sleep"enabledtrue or false switches circadian lighting or sleep. Any other value, such as "on", is refused: nothing changes, and the node logs a warning once per deploy and shows it on its status.
lighting.kind = "schedule"wakeTime, bedTime"HH:MM", either alone. With its own times, a time set this way lasts until a deploy gives the node a different setting for it. While following a house schedule, it is replaced at the house’s next change and at every flow start.
lighting.kind = "startup"NoneSends the style, and with publishing on, its availability, state and discovery. Send it a few seconds after the flows start, as the examples do.
topic = home/<area>/circadian/set, home/<area>/sleep/setON or OFFAs the circadian and sleep messages above. Read only while it publishes. Retained messages and other payloads are ignored.
topic = home/<area>/wake-time/set or bed-time/setA timeA time from Home Assistant, with its own times only.
msg.status from a Status nodeMQTT out’s “connected”Taken as the startup message (msg.lighting = {kind: "startup"}), so the node announces itself again once the broker can take it. Wire in a Status node whose scope is your MQTT out node. Any other status message is ignored.

Switching circadian lighting on takes back the lights a person set, even when it is already on, so a flow can send it to bring lights back into step.

Outputs

The port order is fixed.

Two output ports
PortMessageConnect to
1 · Stylemsg.lighting = {kind: "style", source: "circadian", values: {brightnessPct, colorTempKelvin} | null, transitionSeconds, until, takeBack?}One or more room lighting controllers.
2 · PublicationsAvailability, circadian and sleep state, its own wake and bed times, and discovery.An MQTT out node with no topic set.

It sends a style once the flows start, every Restyle interval, at its startup message, and at once when circadian lighting is switched, or while it is on, when sleep or the times change. Each style lasts three intervals: if the policy stops (deleted, disabled, or its flow stopped), rooms stop styling soon after. Deleted with a modified deploy, it tells rooms at once. While circadian lighting is off it sends a style with no values, and rooms style nothing; sleep and time changes then send no style of their own.

A style is not a request to change a light.

It never switches a light on or off, and never reaches a light that an owner (such as an activity) holds or a person set.

Optional MQTT and Home Assistant

Publishing is off unless switched on. The topics keep the names the room controller used before 6.0, so a flow already writing home/<area>/circadian/set keeps working when this node has that area. Subscribe an MQTT in node to home/<area>/+/set and wire it into the policy to cover all four set topics.

Published topics and incoming requests
TopicDirectionPayload
home/<area>/circadian/availabilityOutput 2Availability.
home/<area>/circadian/state, …/setOutput 2 / MQTT inON or OFF.
home/<area>/sleep/state, …/setOutput 2 / MQTT inON or OFF.
home/<area>/wake-time/state, …/setOutput 2 / MQTT inHH:MM:SS, with its own times only.
home/<area>/bed-time/state, …/setOutput 2 / MQTT inHH:MM:SS, with its own times only.

With both settings on, Home Assistant shows Circadian lighting and Sleep switches and, with its own times, Wake time and Bed time, on the policy’s own device. Before announcing, it clears the circadian entities older room controllers in its area published, migrated or not, in the same batch, so Home Assistant can give the new ones the same entity IDs. See Home Assistant.

Status

While styling, a green dot shows day or sleep with the current brightness and kelvin, for example day · 100% 6500K. With circadian lighting off, a grey dot shows off. When its chosen house schedule is missing, the status adds schedule missing: own times. A refused enable value shows a yellow ring, refused <source> <value>: send true or false, and nothing changes.
