Lighting nodes

House schedule node

Settings for the configuration node that holds one wake and bed time for many rooms. It has no wires: circadian lighting policies choose it as their Schedule.

On this pageOne wake time. One bed time. Many rooms.Where it is chosenSettingsChanging the times
home-house-scheduleconfiguration node · no wires

One wake time. One bed time. Many rooms.

A configuration node, like an MQTT broker: it never appears on the canvas and has no inputs or outputs. Choose it in a circadian lighting policy’s Schedule setting and that policy follows its wake and bed times, hearing of every change as it happens, without wires. In the configuration nodes list it shows its name, or “house schedule”.

Those policies then have no wake and bed time entities of their own in Home Assistant. A policy that keeps its own times (for a child’s room, say) chooses none. Deleting the house schedule in the editor returns its policies to their own times.

Looking for wiring?

Follow Share a house schedule to set one up with several rooms.

Where it is chosen

  • Circadian lighting policy: its Schedule setting. The policy follows this schedule’s times.
  • House schedule MQTT: its Schedule setting. It publishes this schedule’s times and changes them from messages or Home Assistant.

Settings

Defaults below are from the editor definition. Times are HH:MM in the Node-RED host’s local time.

Editor settings and exact defaults
Setting / propertyDefaultMeaning
Namename · stringhouseLabels the schedule and names its topics, home/<name>/schedule/.... Needs a letter or digit.
Wake timewakeTime · string07:00The wake time, from 00:00 to 23:59.
Bed timebedTime · string23:00The bed time, from 00:00 to 23:59; must differ from the wake time.

Changing the times

Times change at runtime from a message or from Home Assistant, through a house schedule MQTT node. A time changed that way lasts until a deploy gives this schedule a different Wake time or Bed time, which then applies. The times in use are kept in the node’s context, so they survive a restart only with a context store configured (see the README’s Recommended setup).

Sources: nodes/house-schedule.html, nodes/house-schedule.ts, lib/house-schedule.ts, README “Room lighting”, examples/House schedule.json.
